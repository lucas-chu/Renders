# Saint Peter's — morning in Vatican City

Deliverables: editable Blender scene; a 30-second, 24 fps, 1920×1080 exterior film; representative stills. Build in metres. Morning light from east/southeast, restrained saturation, open shadows, smooth physical camera movement. No titles or overlays.

Layout: façade at y=0, east towards +y; basilica extends west towards -y. Square centre (0,180), inner oval radii 98 and 74.5 m. Façade 116m wide, 45.5m high. Four rows of colonnade columns and 140 rooftop saints. Main dome rises to about 136m; include lantern, radial ribs, drum windows and paired columns, subsidiary domes, nave and transept. Establish surrounding Vatican palace blocks, Borgo and Via della Conciliazione so views have urban context.

Camera: three ten-second movements: wide establishment; square-level approach with fountain and colonnade parallax; rising three-quarter dome reveal. Verify representative frames before full export.

Priorities: correct silhouette and spatial relationships; convincing light/material response; architectural articulation; close-range ornamental detail. Procedural sculpture and peripheral buildings are approximations, not surveyed replicas. Do not represent the result as a photogrammetric digital twin.
