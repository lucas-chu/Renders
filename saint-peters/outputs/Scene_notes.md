# Morning at Saint Peter’s

A 30-second exterior architectural visualization of Saint Peter’s Basilica and its square, made in Blender 5.2.1.

## Deliverables

- `Saint_Peters_Basilica_30s.mp4` — 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, 30 seconds, silent.
- `Saint_Peters_Basilica.blend` — editable geometry, materials, lighting, three animated cameras, and distance haze. Fonts are embedded; no external textures are needed.

## Sequence

- 0–10 seconds: aerial approach over the square and surrounding Vatican/Borgo buildings.
- 10–20 seconds: lower approach past the fountain, colonnade, and façade.
- 20–30 seconds: rising three-quarter view of the main dome and lantern.

## Reconstruction and limits

The scene uses published architectural dimensions, photographic references, and geographically placed OpenStreetMap building outlines. It includes four ranks of colonnade columns, 140 colonnade statues, the façade’s 13 rooftop figures, the obelisk, twin fountains, subsidiary domes, palace courtyards, and garden trees.

This is a procedural architectural reconstruction, not a photogrammetric replica. Sculptures, ornamental carvings, roof forms, paving patterns, landscape and building elevations are simplified or approximated. Building heights use map tags where available and estimates otherwise. Visitors are static; the camera and water shading are animated. It should not be used as a surveyed or conservation model.
